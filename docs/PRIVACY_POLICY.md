**Who we are.** Gym Empire ("the game") is operated by [COMPANY NAME, ADDRESS, CONTACT EMAIL].

**Game data.** Your progress (lifter name, appearance, in-game stats, gym, items, settings) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect real names, body measurements, health or fitness data. All in-game stats are fictional.

**Advertising.** The game shows optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. On iOS, we respect App Tracking Transparency. You can switch personalized ads off at any time in Settings. AdMob may process device identifiers and IP addresses; see Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

**Purchases.** In-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We receive a purchase receipt, which we use only to verify and deliver the item.

**Online features (future).** If you use leaderboards, your lifter name and game scores will be stored on our servers (Supabase) and shown to other players.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13. Where a child is detected, only non-personalized ads are requested.

**Your rights.** You can delete all local data with Settings › Reset progress, or by uninstalling the game. For online data, contact us at [EMAIL].
